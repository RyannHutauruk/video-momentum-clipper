How it works

Three local pipelines. No external APIs. No data leaves the server.

1. Find the moment

Per-second audio loudness + visual motion scoring. Cheers, punchlines, action beats — they bubble to the top automatically.

2. Cut to 9:16

Each moment is exported as 1080×1920 H.264 + AAC at YouTube-recommended bitrates. Ready to upload, ready to monetize.

3. Caption + hype

Local Whisper transcribes the speech, words are grouped into punchy 2–3-word phrases, burnt in with bold outlines. A scroll-stopping hook lands at second 0.
