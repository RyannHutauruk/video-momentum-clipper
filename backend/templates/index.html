No AI. Just smart audio & motion analysis. We find the most exciting moments, cut them to 9:16, add a hook, and pin a CTA — ready to upload to YouTube Shorts, TikTok & Reels.

How it works

  1. Audio peaks. We extract the audio and score every second by loudness — cheers, impacts, and hype rise to the top.
  2. Motion. OpenCV measures frame-to-frame change. Action-heavy moments get a boost.
  3. Pick & cut. Top non-overlapping windows are exported as 1080×1920 H.264, with a bold hook caption (first 2.5s) and a CTA (last 2.5s).
Built for short-form. No AI integrations — pure ffmpeg + OpenCV + numpy.
